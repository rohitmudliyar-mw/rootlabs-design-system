01 — Strategy

The brain of the brand.

Everything visual and verbal exists to express what is on this page. A few ideas that anchor every decision after.

Mission

Help women 35+ get deep, continuous sleep by fixing the chemistry behind 3 a.m. wakeups, not by sedating them. Most sleep aids get you down. We restore the deep sleep stress takes away.

Archetype — The Sage, with a Caregiver heart

A trusted clinician who has read every study but will not lecture you. We share the truth plainly, and we make it feel supportive, never cold. Knowledgeable, calm, on your side.

Positioning statement

In a sleep category built on melatonin, sedatives, and "knock-out" marketing, AshwaMag stands for the best-absorbed combination of magnesium, built for deep sleep. Magnesium oxide and melatonin can get you to sleep. They do not restore the deep sleep that stress takes away. We do, with a liposomal lipid layer, magnesium glycinate, KSM-66 and L-theanine, no sugar, and third-party testing across 7 certifications.

Core narrative · the one idea to protect

Most magnesium barely absorbs. Most sleep aids just sedate. AshwaMag is the best-absorbed combination of magnesium, built for one thing: deep sleep. We restore it. We never sedate.

05 — The Science

The mechanism is the message.

This is the clinical heart of the brand. Every claim traces back to a dose and a study on this page. When in doubt, explain the biology before the benefit.

The cortisol–magnesium loop

Stress keeps cortisol elevated into the night, and it can surge around 3 a.m. That surge burns through magnesium reserves and fragments deep sleep. Less magnesium means a sharper cortisol spike the next night. The loop tightens. AshwaMag replenishes magnesium so the loop loosens and deep sleep returns.

How liposomal delivery works
1
Lipid shell
Magnesium is encased in a lipid bilayer that mimics the body's fat-soluble carriers.
2
Survives stomach acid
The shell protects the mineral through the gut instead of dumping it all at once.
3
Sustained release
Steady intestinal absorption across roughly 12 hours, with about 17% better uptake.

06 — Imagery Direction

Real people. Clinical calm.

Imagery is where credibility breaks fastest. One fake or fear-driven image undoes everything else in this book. Shoot real. Stay calm.

The hard rule

Never use AI-generated images of people, results, or product. Real women, real product, real settings. This is a trust brand in a category full of stock-photo theatrics.

Style
  • Cool bone and soft green backgrounds. Even, calm, clinical light.
  • Macro of the berry gummy and the lipid-bilayer illustration.
  • Real women 35+, calm and rested. Relatable but premium.
  • Product label always legible. Texture and detail over gloss.
Never
  • The "tired woman clutching her head" cliché.
  • Fear or distress imagery. No darkness as dread.
  • Melatonin-style moon-and-stars sedative tropes.
  • Generic stock that could belong to any supplement.

Acceptable alternative

When there is no real, on-brand photo, type carries the piece. A clean Fraunces headline on bone outperforms a weak stock image every time.

07 — Motion

Calm, never frantic.

Motion should feel like the brand voice: considered and purposeful. It guides the eye toward sleep, not away from it. It is never decoration.

Principles
  • Calm over playful. Subtle fades, gentle slides. No bounce or spring.
  • Easing: ease-out for entrances, ease-in-out for transitions.
  • Duration: 200–400ms for UI, up to ~700ms for section reveals.
  • Highlight reveal: the green highlight may draw in once per view, sparingly.
Never
  • Flashing, rapid strobing, or aggressive parallax.
  • Looping animation that competes with the message.
  • Motion for its own sake.

Honor reduced motion

When a user has prefers-reduced-motion on, replace animation with an instant change or a simple fade. See Accessibility.

10 — Claims & Evidence Integrity

If we can't prove it, we don't say it.

This is a supplement in a regulated, skeptical category. These rules keep every claim defensible and every testimonial honest.

Banned — no exceptions
  • Disease claims. We never say "treats", "cures", or "diagnoses".
  • Fabricated stats, studies, testimonials, or credentials.
  • "Knock you out", "miracle", "guaranteed" sleep language.
  • Implying overnight results. The honest curve is 18–28 days.
Allowed — with proof
  • Structure-function claims tied to the actual dose in the product.
  • Cited study results, with the citation shown alongside.
  • Real reviews, disclosed: "compensated for time, not opinion. Results vary."
  • "#1 on TikTok" and "#1 doctor-recommended", only with the source, metric, and date that back them.
  • Third-party testing and all 7 certifications, referenced by name.
